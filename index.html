About me

The analyst behind the data

I studied Sports Science in Barcelona and spent years as a personal trainer — that's where I first noticed how much data could tell you if you knew how to read it. Tracking performance metrics, analysing training loads, making decisions under pressure: the mindset was already there.

After years moving between projects, a pandemic, and several countries — Menorca, Chicago, Colombia, Mexico — I decided to pivot into data analytics. I completed a bootcamp at Immune Technology Institute in Madrid, then spent five months in the Philippines with my best friend. That trip gave me my first real data project: an end-to-end analysis of Philippine tourism arrivals.

I came back to Spain with a clearer direction, a growing portfolio and the same curiosity I packed for my first trip.

Sales forecasting model for a real healthcare clinic using XGBoost. Multi-source merge: bookings, weather, local events, public holidays. Progression: Linear Reg. (R²=0.61) → XGBoost (R²=0.74) → Enriched XGBoost (R²=0.79).
